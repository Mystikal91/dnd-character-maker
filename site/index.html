Questions

Is it really free?

Yes: no subscription, no ads. It is a passion project, with open code.

Where do my characters go?

On your own device. To move or save them you can use a file or your Google Drive.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, once it has been opened. You can also install it on your phone like an app.

Is it an official D&D product?

No. It uses the SRD 5.1, under a free licence, and is not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.

Does it work with Aurora content?

Yes. You can import the content files (XML) made for Aurora Builder and Aurora Legacy, from a file, an index file or an address. Nothing from Aurora or from any publisher is included: you bring what you already own.
